## Writing rules (from Siraj's master prompt)

Author: Sirajul Islam Sohag, Certified Google Ads Specialist and PPC Consultant, **3+ years** of experience,
Google Ads management, PPC optimization, conversion tracking and lead generation for US home service businesses.
Audience: US business owners, contractors, roofing, remodeling, HVAC and companies looking for Google Ads management.

- Match the real search intent of the primary keyword. Answer the question directly in the first untitled section (2 short paragraphs), useful for Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT Search, Gemini and Perplexity. Never promise rankings or AI citations.
- 1,400 to 2,200 words. Simple, natural, professional US English written for business owners. No generic AI intros ("In today's digital landscape..."), no filler, no repetition, no keyword stuffing. Never use the em dash character; use a hyphen or rewrite.
- H2 sections with descriptive headings; use "### " sub-headings, "- " bullet lists and "1. " numbered lists where they help scanning. Include at least one comparison or checklist and practical recommendations.
- Cover practical, experience-based detail where relevant: Search campaigns, PPC management, keyword targeting and match types, negative keywords and search term reviews, conversion tracking, GA4, Google Tag Manager, call tracking, lead quality, landing pages, ongoing optimization.
- NEVER invent personal stories, client results, case studies or statistics. The only real results allowed are the figures already published in `src/content/case-studies.ts` (quote them exactly and link the case study). General industry facts must be accurate and non-numeric unless backed by an official Google source.
- 3 to 6 internal links using descriptive keyword anchors, including the "Must link to" pages from the plan. Only link to URLs that exist (the checker enforces this). Link previously published posts when relevant.
- 1 to 3 external links, only to authoritative official sources (support.google.com/google-ads, developers.google.com, support.google.com/analytics, skillshop). Verify each URL with web search before using it.
- End with a "Frequently Asked Questions" section (4 to 6 items, each paragraph exactly "**Question?** Answer") and then a short closing CTA section linking to /contact (book a consultation) and one of /services/google-ads-audit or /portfolio. Keep it helpful, not salesy.
- Keep each post genuinely different from earlier posts (different angle, examples and structure). Do not reuse paragraphs.
- metaTitle max 60 chars (use the plan title), metaDescription 120 to 160 chars with the primary keyword, 4 to 6 seo.keywords (primary first), 3 tags, category = the plan's category, excerpt 1 to 2 sentences.
